Customer Support Ticket Priority Prediction and Automated Assignment System using Agentforce

📌 Project Overview

This project is designed to automate customer support ticket management using Salesforce Agentforce. It helps predict the priority of customer support tickets and automatically assigns them to the appropriate support team or agent.

🎯 Objectives

- Predict the priority of customer support tickets.
- Automatically assign tickets to the appropriate agent or team.
- Reduce manual work for support teams.
- Improve ticket response time.
- Provide efficient and organized customer support.

🚀 Key Features

- Customer Support Ticket Management
- Ticket Priority Prediction
- Automated Ticket Assignment
- AI-powered Support Automation
- Agentforce Integration
- Improved Support Workflow

🛠️ Technologies Used

- Salesforce
- Agentforce
- Artificial Intelligence
- Automation
- Customer Support Management

⚙️ Working Process

1. A customer support ticket is created.
2. The ticket details are analyzed.
3. The system predicts the ticket priority.
4. Based on the ticket information, the appropriate agent or team is identified.
5. The ticket is automatically assigned.
6. The support team can process and resolve the ticket efficiently.

💡 Benefits

- Saves time and manual effort.
- Faster ticket assignment.
- Helps prioritize urgent customer issues.
- Improves customer support efficiency.
- Reduces errors in manual assignment.
